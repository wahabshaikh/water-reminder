Someone wants you to drink water 💧

This is Sippy. It does exactly one thing: it nudges you to have a glass of water, and it looks very pleased with you when you do.

How to install

  1. Tap Download Sippy for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded .apk. Android will ask if you trust this source — say yes (it only asks once).
  3. Open Sippy. It'll ask your name and remember it — or come back here and tap Open it to skip the typing.
